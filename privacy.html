Privacy Notice

How Market Support Company Limited collects, uses and protects personal data under Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) (“PDPA”).

Effective date: [1 October 2026]

1. Who we are

Market Support Company Limited (“Market Support”, “we”, “us”) is a market research company based in Bangkok and a member of ESOMAR. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this notice. When we conduct research on behalf of a client, we may also act as a data processor under that client’s instructions.

2. Personal data we collect

Clients, partners and website visitors: name, job title, company, email, phone number, the content of your enquiry, and basic technical data such as IP address and browser type.

Research respondents: contact details used for recruitment and quality checks; demographic and screening information; your answers, opinions and behaviour; and, where you agree, audio, video, photographs, location and time stamps of the interview.

We collect sensitive data, such as health information, only when a study requires it and only with your explicit consent.

3. Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To conduct research, with your consent as a respondent.
  • To check research quality, for example calling back respondents to confirm an interview took place, based on our legitimate interest in accurate data.
  • To answer enquiries, send estimates and deliver projects, as needed to enter into or perform a contract.
  • To pay incentives and keep records, to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We never use research data for selling, marketing or any purpose other than research.

4. Our promise to research respondents

Taking part is always voluntary. You can skip any question or stop at any time without giving a reason. Your identity stays confidential: our clients receive anonymised or combined results. We share identifiable information, such as a recording in which you can be seen, only if you have agreed to it, and only for research purposes. We follow the ESOMAR International Code and obtain consent from a parent or guardian before interviewing anyone under 20 years of age.

5. Who we share personal data with

  • Clients who commission research, in anonymised form unless you have agreed otherwise.
  • Service providers who help us run research, such as survey platforms, cloud storage, recruitment partners, venues, transcribers and translators. All are bound by confidentiality and data protection terms.
  • Government authorities, where the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data.

6. Transfers outside Thailand

Some survey platforms and clients are located abroad. When personal data leaves Thailand, we make sure the destination has adequate protection, or we put appropriate safeguards in place as the PDPA requires.

7. How long we keep it, and how we protect it

We keep identifiable research data only as long as the project and quality checks require, normally no more than 12 months after project completion, after which we delete or anonymise it. We keep business and financial records for as long as Thai law requires. Access to personal data is restricted to authorised staff and protected with technical and organisational security measures.

8. Your rights

Under the PDPA you have the right to:

  • withdraw your consent at any time;
  • access and obtain a copy of your personal data;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • receive or transfer your data to another organisation;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use your data;
  • ask us to delete or anonymise your data; and
  • complain to the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within the time the law allows.
