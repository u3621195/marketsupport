Our Story

Three Decades of Trusted Research

Market Support was established in 1995 with a clear aim: provide local understanding and expertise at an international standard. We started small — with a dedicated team and loyal clients who believed in us.

Today, we've grown into a medium-sized full-service research firm with 30+ full-time professionals, 200–300 part-time interviewers on call, and a track record spanning 20+ industries.

Our practices conform to ESOMAR standards, and our services cover qualitative and quantitative research, desk research, workshops, and advanced analytical techniques.
