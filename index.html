Who we are

Local understanding, international standards, and a genuinely collaborative way of working

Market Support is an independent market research company with deep roots in Thailand and broad experience across Southeast Asia.

Our role is simple: to help clients understand people better, see opportunities more clearly, and move forward with greater confidence.

What we do

Thoughtfully designed research solutions

A warm, practical blend of qualitative understanding, quantitative rigor, analytics, and fieldwork expertise.

Qualitative Research

Thoughtful conversations and real-world context to understand the “why” behind behavior.

  • Focus groups & in-depth interviews
  • Ethnography & home visits
  • Online communities & diaries

Quantitative Research

Robust measurement to validate and scale insights with fit-for-purpose methods.

  • Online & offline surveys
  • CLT & home use tests
  • CATI & face-to-face interviews

Data & Analytics

Analysis that turns data into direction and supports clearer business decisions.

  • Segmentation & targeting
  • Perceptual mapping & conjoint
  • Driver analysis & modeling

Fieldwork & Facilities

Trusted execution, strong QC, and comfortable spaces for discussion and observation.

  • Nationwide fieldwork capability
  • Structured quality control
  • Virtual and in-person viewing options
