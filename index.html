Rigorous fieldwork and QC, so you can rest assured

Our own field operations run studies of any complexity, nationwide, on time and without cutting corners. Our QC team checks at least 50% of each interviewer’s questionnaires on every project.

30%

telephone call-back QC

20%

area QC in person or on site

Plus a separate trained team for telephone interviews (CATI) and a dedicated team for the medical and healthcare channel.

  1. 1Interviewer briefing

    The researcher on your project briefs the field team on the study and questionnaire before any interview starts.

  2. 2Supervised field team

    A headquarters supervisor and area field supervisors manage every study.

  3. 3On-the-spot QC

    Supervisors observe interviews live: interviewing practice, use of materials and manner.

  4. 4Telephone QC

    We call respondents back to confirm the interview took place as reported and re-check selected answers.

  5. 5Data checks & cleaning

    Every response is edited for completeness and logic, and checked for interview length, time stamp and location.
